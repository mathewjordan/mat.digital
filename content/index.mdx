## Work

At Northwestern University Libraries, I work with an outstanding team creating projects that support digital humanities, collections, and scholarship. Before joining Northwestern, I honed my identity as a designer and developer in academia, working at the University of Tennessee Libraries and, prior to that, on projects for Duke, NC State, Wake Forest, Butler, SUNY Albany, and others.

[Reach out](mailto:mat@northwestern.edu) if you'd like to collaborate on digital humanities, collections, or scholarship projects.
