## About

I’m a designer and developer at Northwestern University, building open-source tools for digital humanities, collections, and scholarship. When not ruminating in the office, I’m likely avoiding technology; hands in the dirt; feet on a trail; a book in one hand, a spatula in the other; or trying, always, to be a better partner and parent.

</Section>

## Work

At Northwestern University Libraries, I work with an outstanding team on open-source tools for digital humanities, collections, and scholarship. Before joining Northwestern, I honed my identity as a designer and developer in academia, working at the University of Tennessee Libraries and, prior to that, on projects for Duke, NC State, Wake Forest, Butler, SUNY Albany, and others.

[Reach out](mailto:mat@northwestern.edu) if you'd like to collaborate on digital humanities, collections, or scholarship projects.
